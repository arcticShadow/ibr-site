Finding us

The workshop is in the Hillside Road light industrial area in Glenfield, on Auckland's North Shore — the pocket sometimes addressed as Wairau Valley. It's a workshop, not a showroom: there are usually boats outside.

Bringing a boat

Bring it on the trailer if you can. If you want the trailer looked at as well, say so when you ring so we can allow the time.

If you can't get it here

We offer collection and delivery, including to marinas, and a mobile service for larger non-trailerable RIBs. Tell us where the boat is and what the access is like.
